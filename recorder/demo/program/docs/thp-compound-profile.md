# Project Theophylline — compound profile

*Fictional example for the kton recorder.*

- Class: methylxanthine; bronchodilator, weak anti-inflammatory effect.
- Absorption: complete after oral dosing; absorption rate varies with formulation and food.
- Distribution: volume about 0.5 L/kg.
- Elimination: hepatic (CYP1A2); half-life about 8 h in adult non-smokers, shorter in smokers.
- Range: about 5–15 mg/L; toxicity (nausea, arrhythmia, seizures) increases above 20 mg/L.
